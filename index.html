SLA targets used

  • Kick-off call within 2 business days of handover (≥90% adherence)
  • Implementation-ready to go-live: 1–2 products ≤3 business days, 3–4 products ≤5, more than 4 products ≤15 (≥90% within SLA)

How these figures are calculated

  • An implementation is a property plus opportunity, so a stack counts once; products = distinct products. Abandoned contracts are excluded.
  • Implementation-ready = last PRS received from the customer; the SLA clock runs in business days (Mon–Fri) from that date to the actual release date.
  • Active = first PRS received by week end and not yet live. On track / At risk / Delayed compare elapsed business days with the SLA limit (At risk after 80% of it). Awaiting client = PRS still incomplete (Blocked). On hold = paused contracts.
  • Past weeks are rebuilt from dates in the 16 Nov file, not from weekly snapshots; pause status is as of today. Each week sits in the month where most of its days fall. Week 16–22 Nov is partial.
  • Kick-off call is not in the files, so it shows no data. Status: On track ≥90%, At risk 80–89%, Breach <80%. Arrows show change against the previous week.

Go-live = Contract Status “Live”. Created Date is used as the sale date. ACV exists only in the Arnika file: Under-setup counts differ by 4 between the two files (459 on 13 Nov vs 455 on 16 Nov).
